Quotes by product

Number of quotes that include each product. A quote with storefront and glass counts in both. Click a product to filter the page.

How status is set

  • Dropped off: 3 or more quotes, none in the last 60 days of data.
  • Slowing: 3 or more quotes in the 6 months before the last 3 full months, and the last-3-month pace is 25% or more below it.
  • Growing: same test, 25% or more above.
  • New: first quote (for the selected product, when one is picked) in the last 3 full months.
  • Steady: regular quoting within 25% of the earlier pace.
  • Occasional: fewer than 4 quotes, still active.
  • Light, inactive: 1 or 2 quotes, none in the last 60 days.
